# Release 2026-07-28.2

Status: active Phase 4 Wave 2 conditional-coverage release

Release `2026-07-28.2` preserves `2026-07-28.1` and adds two experimental partial-coverage
criteria: School education quality and Research and innovation ecosystem.

## Scope

- Stable universe: 91 countries.
- Published observations and scores: 1,080 each.
- Explicit criterion-country outcomes: 1,092.
- Published criteria: twelve; eleven ready and eight global-core.
- Previous release: `2026-07-28.1`.

  `sha256:de67681265428de36f6e182cb9435140fd2bb7ff5b5a284b4fed9a97fd5451de`.

## School education quality

The experimental criterion freezes the World Bank HCI+ learning-adjusted years of schooling
construct. It has 88 valid countries: Bahamas and Bolivia are missing and Guyana is stale. The
earlier 87/91 research result required three HCI+ fields; production no longer rejects Antigua and
Barbuda merely because an unrelated published schooling-component field is absent.

## Research and innovation ecosystem

The experimental criterion uses WIPO's published Global Innovation Index 2025 Innovation outputs
sub-index. It deliberately excludes the GII input sub-index to avoid duplicating governance,
infrastructure, education, and market inputs already represented elsewhere. Coverage is 85/91;
Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Fiji, Grenada, Guyana, and Haiti are missing.

Both criteria use `CONDITIONAL_COMPLETE_CASE`, activate at raw weight 0.6 or above, and never
impute or issue a final score for a non-valid country.

## Held candidates

not commercially clean. Social-protection system reach, Food-safety system capacity, and Freedom
of expression and religion still need exact source/construct gates described in the
[Wave 2 report](../../../data/reports/phase4-wave2-2026-07-28/report.md).
