## Role

This additive Phase 5G release preserves C66 and introduces C67 **Projected warm-day frequency
(2030)** as the second real locality-derived criterion. The previous broad name
**Long-term climate-change exposure** is retained only as a historical name.

## C67

- Publisher: European Commission, Joint Research Centre.
- Asset: GHS-UCDB R2024A V1.2 climate archive.
- Field: `CL_WDS_245_2030`.
- Construct: projected percentage of warm days in 2021-2030 under SSP2-4.5.
- Baseline: calendar-day 90th percentile over 1961-1990.
- Model: EC-Earth3.
- Locality universe: 388 GHSL urban centres across 89 supported countries.
- Country aggregation: mean of the two highest-scoring eligible cities, minimum one.
- Coverage: PCC, 89/91; Antigua and Barbuda and Grenada have no qualifying locality.
- Scope: locality-derived.
- Status: ready, default-enabled, experimental.
