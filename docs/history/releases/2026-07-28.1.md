## Overall job-market opportunity

The criterion uses the International Labour Organization's November 2025 ILOSTAT modelled
estimates. It combines the 2025 employment-to-population ratio, labour-force participation rate,
and reverse-direction unemployment rate as equal-weight average-rank percentiles. Projections for
2026 and 2027 are deliberately excluded.

Coverage is 88/91. Antigua and Barbuda and Grenada have no source record; Ukraine's latest complete
source year is 2021 and is stale. The criterion therefore uses `CONDITIONAL_COMPLETE_CASE`, becomes
active at raw weight 0.6, and never imputes or renormalises a country-specific partial score.
