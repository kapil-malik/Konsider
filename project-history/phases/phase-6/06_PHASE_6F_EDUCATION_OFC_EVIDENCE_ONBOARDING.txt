3. one of the frozen output-and-breadth combinations passes.

Otherwise, complete represented-country evidence is `STRONG_SIGNAL_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

A country absent from the retained research-intensive source universe is
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
