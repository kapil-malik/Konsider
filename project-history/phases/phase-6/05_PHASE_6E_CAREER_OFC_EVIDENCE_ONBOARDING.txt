THRESHOLD POLICY

Implement the frozen Phase 6B.1 P60 rule:

- scale >= P60 AND share >= P60; OR
- scale >= P80 AND share >= P40; OR
- share >= P80 AND scale >= P40.

Use frozen raw thresholds derived from the approved reference pool.

Do not recompute thresholds from the currently available countries during each run.

Retain:

- policy/version ID;
- raw thresholds per route/construct;
- reference distribution identity;
- P50/P55/P60/P65/P70 research sensitivity links;
- no country-specific exceptions.

CONFIDENCE AND PRECEDENCE

Implement Phase 6D confidence contracts and the accepted precedence:

1. recent harmonised observed;
2. recent official national observed with strong mapping;
3. harmonised modelled;
4. supplemental.

Rules:

- high-confidence complete evidence may establish positive or not-established;
- medium-confidence evidence may establish positive;
- a medium-confidence negative requires defensible mapping completeness;
- low-confidence supplemental evidence cannot establish a public state on its own;
- contradictions are retained and reason-coded;
- observed evidence is not overwritten by modelled evidence.
