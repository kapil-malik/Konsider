# India

- Large technology labor market and startup ecosystem, especially in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, NCR, and Mumbai.
- Cost of living can be attractive compared with most Phase 1 countries, but premium urban housing and schooling vary widely.
- Air quality is a recurring concern in several major cities and should be treated as a major family-context caveat.
- Female safety and general safety are highly location-dependent and require city-level research.
- Higher education includes elite institutions, but quality and fit vary sharply across the broader system.
